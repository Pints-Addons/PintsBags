Pint's Bags - Changelog

v1.1.4
- WoW Forever: the bank now shows your bank tabs (the 48-slot tabs you buy at the banker). Before, it showed the keyring
  and said the bank was full.
- WoW Forever: until you've bought your first bank tab, the game's own bank window opens so you can buy one.

v1.1.3
- WoW Forever: right-click a potion, food or other usable item in the bags to use it (out of combat).
- WoW Forever: no more "blocked" errors in combat when the bag window updates.
- The bank opens in Pint's Bags; Blizzard's own bank window stays out of the way.

v1.1.2
- WoW Forever: right-clicking items to use or sell them no longer gets blocked (Blizzard's own bag windows are tucked
  away once at login instead of being closed each time).
- WoW Forever: the reagent bag is off-white - its edge, and its empty slots get a light wash - so it stands apart.

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v1.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Hides Blizzard's own combined bag so the two don't overlap.
- The reagent bag is included, with its slot in the bag bar and in one-big-bag view.
- Right-clicking an item to use it no longer gets blocked.
- Bank bags follow the modern client's numbering.

v1.0
- First public release.
- All your bags in one window, grouped by kind (equipment, consumables, trade goods, quest items...) or as
  one big bag with every slot in bag order.
- Search by name, type, slot, quality or "bound"; sort button (uses the game's sort when it has one).
- Optional colour for each bag, quality borders, quest item marks, item level on gear, new-item glow, fade junk.
- The bank in its own window, with buying bank bag slots.
- With Pint's Alts: any character's bags and bank from anywhere, and gold across the realm.
