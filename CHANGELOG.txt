Pint's Bags - Changelog

v1.1.11
- Smarter sort: the sort button now groups items by what they're for - potions, bandages, scrolls, cooking (food with its
  ingredients), cloth, leather, ore/bars/stone, herbs, gems, elemental, enchanting, engineering parts, other trade goods,
  reagents, quest items, recipes, gear (by slot), bags, ammo, keys - then by quality and level, junk last. The game's
  own sort (which only puts like items together) is now off by default; "Use the game's sort" in options brings it back.
- WoW Forever: fixed an error when a fight started ("windows" a nil value), which then caused "blocked" errors
  (SetScale, Hide) as the bags updated mid-fight. In a fight the bags now only repaint until it ends.

v1.1.10
- WoW Forever: right-click items in a fight even when you open your bags mid-fight. Your bag key and the minimap /
  Button Bin button open the bags through a secure toggle, and when a fight starts the use buttons are set over every
  slot, bags open or closed. In a fight, close the bags with the bag key, the minimap button or the X (Escape waits
  until the fight ends). Right-click the minimap button for options as before.
- Fixed right-click sometimes not using items after clicking on the bag window (the window came to the front, over
  the buttons that use items).
- WoW Forever bank: buy bank bag slots by clicking the next locked slot - the game's own "buy this slot" box comes up
  (the click is passed to the game's own purchase button, the only thing the game lets buy). The price shows after
  the slots. Put bags in and take them out of the bank's bag slots.
- WoW Forever: two currency spots on the bottom edge, spaced evenly between the free-slot count and the gold -
  Merchant's Favor and Honor Points to start. Hover either for every character's amount and the account total; click
  one to show a different currency there (or leave it empty).
- Junk mark: a small coin in the top-right corner of grey items and anything on Pint's Junk's quick list (a red cross
  for items it's set to destroy).

v1.1.6
- WoW Forever: right-click potions and other usable items in the bags during a fight. Have your bags open when the
  fight starts: until it ends the bag window holds still (no moving or rearranging) and stays open, so right-click
  always uses the item you clicked. Bags opened mid-fight can't use items until it ends.

v1.1.5
- WoW Forever: right-click to use items (potions, Fire Sapta and so on) no longer stops working after you talk to an NPC.
- WoW Forever: in combat, right-clicking a bag slot does nothing instead of showing a "blocked" error (use the action bar
  mid-fight for now).

v1.1.4
- WoW Forever: the bank now shows your bank tabs (the 48-slot tabs you buy at the banker). Before, it showed the keyring
  and said the bank was full.
- WoW Forever: until you've bought your first bank tab, the game's own bank window opens so you can buy one.

v1.1.3
- WoW Forever: right-click a potion, food or other usable item in the bags to use it (out of combat).
- WoW Forever: no more "blocked" errors in combat when the bag window updates.
- The bank opens in Pint's Bags; Blizzard's own bank window stays out of the way.

v1.1.2
- WoW Forever: right-clicking items to use or sell them no longer gets blocked (Blizzard's own bag windows are tucked
  away once at login instead of being closed each time).
- WoW Forever: the reagent bag is off-white - its edge, and its empty slots get a light wash - so it stands apart.

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v1.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Hides Blizzard's own combined bag so the two don't overlap.
- The reagent bag is included, with its slot in the bag bar and in one-big-bag view.
- Right-clicking an item to use it no longer gets blocked.
- Bank bags follow the modern client's numbering.

v1.0
- First public release.
- All your bags in one window, grouped by kind (equipment, consumables, trade goods, quest items...) or as
  one big bag with every slot in bag order.
- Search by name, type, slot, quality or "bound"; sort button (uses the game's sort when it has one).
- Optional colour for each bag, quality borders, quest item marks, item level on gear, new-item glow, fade junk.
- The bank in its own window, with buying bank bag slots.
- With Pint's Alts: any character's bags and bank from anywhere, and gold across the realm.
