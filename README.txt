Pint's Bags v1.0 - all your bags in one window
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Works on its own.
  Optional:  Pint's Alts - see any character's bags and bank from anywhere, plus gold across the realm

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsBags
  Restart the game.

HOW TO USE
  B or Shift-B opens your bags (Pint's Bags takes them over), /pbag, or the minimap button.
  The bank opens in its own window when you visit a banker.

  - Two views (button at the top, or in options):
      Grouped by kind - equipment, consumables, trade goods, recipes, quest items, ammo, keys...
      One big bag - every slot in bag order, like the default bags in one window
  - Search: click the spyglass or just start typing - matches name, type, slot, quality ("epic") or "bound".
  - Sort button (uses the game's own bag sort when there is one).
  - Combine stacks in the grouped view (your bags aren't changed).
  - Quality borders, quest item marks, item level on gear, glow on new items, fade junk.
  - Optional colour for each bag, so you can see at a glance which bag a slot is in.
  - Free-space count per bag type (drop an item there to put it in that kind of bag).
  - Gold on the realm in the tooltip (with Pint's Alts).
  - With Pint's Alts: pick any character at the top to see their bags or bank, from anywhere.

OPTIONS
  /pbag options, the gear button on the bag window, or right-click the minimap button.
  Slots across, group columns, slot size, gap, window scale, colours, lock position, and whether
  Pint's Bags replaces the default bags and bank.
  Using Bagnon? The options have a "Switch Bagnon off" button (or /pbag off-bagnon).

COMMANDS
  /pbag            open or close your bags
  /pbag bank       open the bank window (your last-seen bank, from anywhere)
  /pbag sort       sort your bags
  /pbag options    options
  /pbag minimap    show or hide the minimap button
